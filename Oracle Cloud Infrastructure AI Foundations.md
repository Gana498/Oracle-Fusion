# Oracle 2025 Cloud Infrastructure AI Foundations

## Why AI Skills Matter
- AI is reshaping work and the labor market.
- Companies using AI effectively will gain a competitive edge.
- Managers prioritize hiring candidates with AI skills.
- AI helps employees overcome career limitations.

## Key Trends (Microsoft & LinkedIn Survey)
1. **AI Adoption at Work**
   - 75% of knowledge workers use AI.
   - 46% started using AI within the last 6 months.

2. **AI Aptitude Over Experience**
   - 66% of leaders won’t hire without AI skills.
   - 71% prefer less experienced candidates with AI skills.

3. **AI is Mainstream**
   - Professionals across all roles (business, software, creative) are skilling up in AI.

## Target Audience
- Cloud engineers and architects wanting to learn AI.
- Beginners with no prior AI/ML experience.
- Suitable for all roles: business, software, creative.

## Course Structure
### Two Main Domains:
1. **AI Stack**
   - **AI**: Machines imitating human intelligence.
   - **Machine Learning**: Learning from data to predict outcomes.
   - **Deep Learning**: Neural networks for complex data.
   - **Generative AI**: Creating new content (e.g., text, images).

2. **Oracle AI Stack**
   - Built on Oracle’s AI infrastructure.
   - Integrated with Oracle products: Database, Autonomous DB, MySQL Heatwave, ERP, HCM, CX.
   - Seamless experience, not DIY.

### 7 Modules Total
- **Modules 1–4**: AI Stack (AI, ML, DL, Gen AI, LLMs).
- **Modules 5–7**: Oracle AI Stack (overview, Gen AI services, Oracle AI services).
